Outdoor equipment enclosure

IP66 forecourt controller housing

A die-cast enclosure for electronics living outdoors at 45 °C ambient and washdown pressure. I set the parting line to keep the gasket groove in one half, sized the compression on the seal for a 20-year set, and added internal ribbing that doubled as the PCB standoff pattern — deleting eight fasteners from the assembly.

02

Handheld product

Moulded terminal housing

Two-shell ABS/PC housing with a live-hinge battery door and an over-moulded grip. The whole design was driven by getting a 1.2 m drop survival without a boot: I moved the wall section to 2.4 mm, used a stepped shut line to take the impact into shear, and validated it against 30 drops on the first tool trial.

03

Structures

Sheet-metal instrument chassis

A folded and spot-welded 1.5 mm chassis carrying a 14 kg instrument stack. The interesting part was the tolerance stack: eleven features between the mounting datum and the connector cut-out. Re-datuming to a single pierced hole pattern pulled the worst case from ±1.9 mm to ±0.6 mm and removed the rework step entirely.

Thermal

Passive heatsink for a sealed unit

28 W dissipated inside a sealed box with no fan. I worked the fin pitch and the internal conduction path together — a bonded thermal pad to the enclosure wall doing most of the work — and got junction temperature 19 °C below limit. Verified with thermocouples against the model before tooling was cut.

Sheet 4 — Process

How a job runs

Written as a revision block, because that is genuinely how the work moves: each stage has an output someone downstream signs for.

RevStageOutput released
ADefineRequirements and constraints agreed in writing — loads, environment, cost per unit, volume, the dates that cannot move.
BConceptThree routes, sketched and costed, with the trade-off made explicit. One gets chosen; the other two get recorded so nobody re-proposes them in month four.
CDetail designFull CAD model, tolerance stack, material and finish selection, DFM review with the supplier who will actually make it.
DValidatePrototypes and the test evidence that they meet the spec — drop, seal, thermal, life. Failures documented, not smoothed over.
ETransferReleased drawing pack, BOM, assembly instructions and first-article inspection. The design leaves my desk buildable by someone else.
